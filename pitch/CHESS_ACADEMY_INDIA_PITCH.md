## 1. Executive Summary & Market Context

India is experiencing an unprecedented chess revolution. Following the historic **Double Gold at the 2024 Chess Olympiad** and the rise of prodigies like **D. Gukesh, R. ### The Academy Bottleneck
* **The Post-Game Review Problem:** Young players (800–1800 Elo) play 20–50 games every week on Chess.com and Lichess. To improve, each game *must* be reviewed.
* **The Coach Capacity Crunch:** A single coach typically manages 20–40 students. Giving detailed, 20-minute post-game analysis for each student's weekly tournament games is mathematically impossible.
* **The "Engine Trap":** When kids check Stockfish on their own, they see `+2.8`, `Blunder: Nd5`, and a jagged eval chart. They don't understand *why* Nd5 failed, what positional concept was violated, or what to drill next.
* **Parent Expectations:** Parents paying ₹2,000 to ₹10,000/month want visible progress and structured homework, not just vague verbal feedback after class.

## 2. The Solution: Chessmasters AI Academy Suite

**Chessmasters** acts as an AI Grandmaster Assistant for academy coaches:

1. **Instant Narrative Post-Game Reports (in plain English):**
   Students or coaches paste any Lichess or Chess.com game URL. In 15 seconds, Chessmasters generates a structured coaching breakdown:
   * **Strengths:** Tactical motifs executed accurately.
   * **Weaknesses:** Root causes of mistakes (e.g. "Rushing queen moves before kingside castling").
   * **Turning Point & Critical Positions:** Embedded interactive boards highlighting the exact moment the advantage swung.
   * **Psychology Panel:** Identifies tilt, time trouble rushes, and overconfidence in winning positions.

2. **Automated, Targeted Homework Drills:**
   Instead of coaches manually looking up puzzle sets, Chessmasters maps each student’s actual blunders directly into verified Lichess training motifs (e.g., *Deflection, Fork, Pinned Knight, Endgame Passed Pawns*).

3. **Batch Tournament Review:**
   After a weekend academy tournament, coaches can submit up to 5 games at once per student and receive an instant aggregated report of recurring habits.

4. **Parent Delight & Retention:**
   Shareable, beautifully formatted coaching reports that students and parents can read anytime on mobile or desktop.
