Strength: the pieces arrived before the attack

From move 6 every piece went to its best square: Bc4 and Qb3 pointed at f7, Nc3 held the centre, and Bg5 pinned the knight before a single pawn moved on the kingside.

“When the sacrifices came, they were not gambles: they were the conclusion of a position already won.”

Focus area: exchange sacrifices: give a rook to remove the last defender, because the eighth rank is worth more than the material.
