1 34. Rc1 Cost: the game

YouWhy is Ra8 mate and Rc1 isn't?
Coach The black king is on g8 with f7, g7 and h7 pawns untouched. Those pawns are the whole wall — none of them can move back, so the eighth rank is a corridor the king cannot leave. White's rook on a8 attacks along that corridor, and the king has no square to step to: f7, g7 and h7 are blocked by its own pawns.

Rc1 attacks nothing on the back rank, so the king is simply never in check.
2 27. Ne7 Cost: a pawn

You moved the knight to a square where it does nothing while a royal fork sat on the board.

What was available: one knight move hits both the king and the rook — a royal fork.
