**Subject:** Unrelated chess site using a near-identical name to our production
domain (deceptive prize marketing)

**Deployment URL:** https://chessmasterss.vercel.app

**Description:**

The deployment at `chessmasterss.vercel.app` is unrelated to our organisation.
Our product is **Chessmasters**, a chess game analysis tool at
**https://www.chessmasterss.in** (registered March 2025; Google Search Console
verified; users in India and the US).

The deployment's subdomain (`chessmasterss`) is confusingly similar to our
production domain (`chessmasterss.in`), and it presents itself as a chess
product: "Play Chess Online Free in India | Chess Masters" with claims of
"real money", prizes, tournaments, and an ELO leaderboard. We have no
relationship with the operator, and we cannot find verifiable operator or
company details on the page.

What we are asking:

1. Please review the deployment against Vercel's Acceptable Use Policy,
   including any misleading or deceptive content representations ("real money"
   and prize claims without disclosed operator identity).
2. If the deployment is an abandoned preview or similar, please consider
   taking it down or renaming the subdomain, as its proximity to
   `chessmasterss.in` causes brand confusion that users have already reported
   to us.

We are not claiming ownership of the word "chessmasters" in general, and we
hold no registered trademark. Our report is based on the name proximity to our
live commercial domain and the potentially deceptive marketing claims on the
page, not a trademark claim.

**Evidence:**

- Deployment: https://chessmasterss.vercel.app (title: "Play Chess Online Free
  in India | Chess Masters"; claims include "real money", prizes, tournaments).
- Our production domain: https://www.chessmasterss.in.
- **Search confusion is measurable:** on 11 October 2026, a brand-name search
  ("chessmasterss") surfaced the unrelated vercel.app deployment (and its
  third-party traffic statistics page) while our own domain did not appear —
  evidence that the name proximity causes active user-facing confusion.
- Our product listing (name, description, pricing for comparison):
  https://caimpare.ai/tool/78319 and https://www.nicheloom.com/launches/45694/.
